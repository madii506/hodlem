FAQ
Questions.

    

      
Did Satoshi really put poker in Bitcoin?
Yes. Bitcoin v0.1's source has a "Poker Lobby" window (Open New Table) and a "Poker" window with Deal Hand, Fold, Call, Raise and Leave Table buttons, plus Deal Me Out and pre-action boxes. The game logic was never written. See uibase.h.

      
Is this gambling?
No. Every chip is play money. You start with 50 play BTC, the first block reward, and you can always Generate Coins again. Nothing here can be bought, sold or cashed out.

      
How do I play inside X?
Post or share the site link. X turns it into a player card: tap play and the table opens inside the post. Keys work too: D deal, F fold, C call, R raise, L leave.

      
What happens to the creator fees?
All $HODLEM creator fees are used to buy $HODLEM and burn it. The Burn window above does it in one transaction the creator wallet signs, and the Burns list reads every burn from the chain. Anyone can also buy and burn with their own SOL.

      
How is "burned" counted?
Every pump.fun coin starts with 1,000,000,000 tokens. Burned is that minus today's on-chain supply, so it counts every burn by anyone, not just ours.

      
Who is the bot?
At the Genesis table it sits as 1A1zP1…DivfNa, the address that got Bitcoin's first 50 coins. Later tables are named after the halving blocks. It plays real poker math: Monte Carlo equity, pot odds, a few bluffs.

      
Is this affiliated with Bitcoin or Satoshi?
No. HODLEM is a fan project and a memecoin. The Bitcoin v0.1 code is © 2009 Satoshi Nakamoto under the MIT license; the windows here are recreated from it.
